# Role: story writer (German script of one clip)

Read `CHECKLIST.md`, `IDEAS.md` and the clip's `projects/<name>/SCRIPT.md` template first.
- Topic: a TRUE story where someone does something that looks stupid, people laugh, it seems to fail, and physics or
  facts prove them right. One picture you can show in the first second. Pitch three when asked, then let Arman pick.
- Facts: every key fact with a source link; tick the primary-source box only when you checked one; mark what is only
  secondary (and if the payoff rests on it, say so loudly).
- Short (51-70 s): paradox hook in sentence 1 (10-16 words), setting, 3-6 escalating beats with 2+ quoted lines, a
  "Moment, ...?" question, "Die Sache ist die:" with an everyday analogy ("Kennst du das ...?"), payoff as the last
  words (10 words or less). Long (6-12 min): the template in `projects/_template_long/SCRIPT.md` (cold open, chapters
  with open loops, a re-hook every 60-90 s, one blank-line paragraph per shot of 6-25 words).
- The narrator talks as "ich" and has opinions. At most one number per sentence. Every line drawable with a prop.
- Run `python3 scripts/script_check.py projects/<name>` until every rule says ok.
- Report in under 150 words: title, hook, secondary-only facts, checker output.
