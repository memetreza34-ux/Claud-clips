## Structure (long form of the short formula)
1. **Cold open** (first 20-40 s): the most absurd moment of the whole story shown first, stated as a fact, then
   "Wie konnte das passieren? Dafür müssen wir zurück nach ..." No logo, no "in diesem Video".
2. **Chapters** (3-6, each 1-2 min, each a mini short): a setting line, escalation with quoted doubters, a "Moment, ...?"
   question that is answered only in the NEXT chapter (open loop), a joke beat every 20-30 s.
3. **Re-hook every 60-90 s**: a new question, a reveal or a cliffhanger ("Und genau da ging alles schief.").
4. **"Die Sache ist die:"** explanation chapter with one everyday analogy ("Kennst du das ...?") and a diagram scene.
5. **Payoff**: the ironic twist, then one line that loops back to the cold open; end on the last word.
Every paragraph needs its own place or prop; reuse sets across chapters (same office, new props, new light).
