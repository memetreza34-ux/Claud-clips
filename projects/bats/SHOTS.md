# "The Bat Bomb That Burned Its Own Base" - shot plan v3 (German)

30 fps, 2003 frames (66.8 s), timed to `voice/words.json` of the German voice (Arman's cloned voice). Frame numbers are word starts (`s * 30`, rounded).
Rules: every sentence is shown literally; every reaction lands 0-3 frames before its word; 6-8 frame crossfade on place
changes, hard cuts only on jumps and match cuts; every hold creeps; light day -> golden -> night blue for the explanation.
Everything that matters stays inside the 9:16 safe area (`CHECKLIST.md`).

## Camera style (v2, from the reference channel)
- **Close by default.** Faces fill at least a third of the frame. A wide shot only to show a place once, then cut in.
- **POV shots.** We look through Lytle's eyes (windshield, letter in his hands, fridge door) with a soft blurred foreground.
- **Reaction zoom.** On every joke beat: fast push onto a face, dark vignette and speed lines, 10-15 frames, hard cut out.
- **Faces carry the joke.** Big eyes and brows, one clear expression per shot (deadpan, panic, smug). The line is the setup, the face is the punch.
- **Depth.** Something blurred in front of the camera in most shots (shoulder, lamp, bat wing).

## Cast
- **Lytle**: small dentist, round glasses, white coat, dental mirror in his pocket. Expressions: amazed, scheming grin, panic.
- **The President**: seen from behind or as hands and a raised eyebrow over the letter (generic, no likeness).
- **Bats**: round, big ears, sleepy eyes; one hero bat with a tiny helmet. Expressions: asleep, wide awake, smug.
- **General**: big moustache, medals. Expressions: proud, then frozen.
- **Clerk**: deadpan typist at the report.
- **Cat**: orange, wide eyes.
