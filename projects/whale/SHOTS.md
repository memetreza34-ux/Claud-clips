# "The Exploding Whale" - shot plan v1 (German)

30 fps, about 1890 frames (63 s), timed to `voice/words.json` (Arman's cloned voice). Frame numbers are word starts.
Look: the 2.5D style of the bat short (`src/videos/batsp`): a painted plate per place (`plates.json`, local
Z-Image-Turbo), our own Pen characters and props in front, calm camera with small pushes on the beats.
Light: grey Oregon day -> golden/dusk after the blast -> night blue for the explanation (shots 15-16) -> dusk.

## Cast
- **Thornton**: engineer, white shirt, yellow hard hat. Expressions: confident grin, thumbs up, never worried.
- **The veteran**: stout, brown work jacket, olive cap, moustache. Expressions: stern, deadpan.
- **The woman**: teal dress, calm, points up while everyone cheers.
- **Crowd**: 1970s colours, cheering, then running.
- **Seagull**: white, grey wings; faints from the smell, later turns its back.
