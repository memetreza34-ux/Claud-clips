# "The Pigeons Who Flew a Bomb" - shot plan v1

30 fps, 2131 frames (71.0 s), timed to `voice/words.json`. Frame numbers are word starts (`s * 30`, rounded).
Rules from the template: every sentence is shown literally; every reaction lands 0-3 frames before its word;
6-8 frame crossfade on place changes, hard cuts only on jumps and match cuts; every hold creeps in 0.15-0.35 %/f,
0.6 %/f on punch words; white 4-frame flash after the hook. Machine-readable frames: `shots.json`.

## Cast
- **Fred**: 1940s scientist, glasses, suit and lab coat. Expressions: thinking, idea, proud, stern.
- **Three pigeons**: same body, told apart by a tiny pilot cap (lead), a scarf, and a dent in the feathers (the stubborn third).
- **Committee and officers**: three grown-ups at a table, reused with uniforms in the office shots.
- **Ground crew**: two mechanics at the airfield.
- **Phone player**: a present-day teen on a sofa.
- **Fred's daughter**: an older woman at the lectern (generic cartoon, no likeness), plus a framed portrait card of Fred.

## Effect cards
Dollar bills, money bag, idea sparkle, light ray, speed lines, seed burst, small rain cloud, dust puff, stamp impact,
flying calendar pages, trophy glints, olive branch.
