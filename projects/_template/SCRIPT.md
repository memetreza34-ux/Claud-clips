## Story formula
1. **Hook** (sentence 1, 10-16 words): the paradox stated as a fact, no setup.
2. **Setting** (1-2 lines, present tense): year, place, a named everyman, one concrete number.
3. **Escalation** (3-6 lines): every line raises the stakes. Doubters mock in short quoted lines. Do not explain the mechanism yet.
4. **Climax:** one scene where it all comes to a head (a demo, a test, a crash). The picture shows it big.
5. **Mystery:** "Wait, ...?"
6. **Pivot:** "You see, ..." then the real mechanism through an everyday analogy ("Ever ...?").
7. **Payoff** (last 1-2 sentences, the last one 10 words or less): the ironic twist. The video ends on the last word.

Every line needs its own place or prop (plan it in `SHOTS.md`). At most one number per sentence.
